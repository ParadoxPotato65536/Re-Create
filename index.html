Why it matters

Small decisions, kept out of the bin

Rethink, don't just recycle

Recycling breaks materials down. Reusing keeps them whole — and usually takes less time than you'd think.

Ideas beat guesswork

Most reusable materials end up in the bin because nobody had five minutes to think of what else they could be.

Built for real bins

Every idea on Re-Create starts with materials people actually throw away, not rare or specialty items.

FAQ

Good to know

Is Re-Create free to use?

Yes. Every material and idea on the site is free to browse — no account needed.

How are the ideas picked?

Each one is a project that's achievable with basic tools and a bit of patience — nothing that needs specialist equipment.

What if I don't have the exact material shown?

Most ideas work with close substitutes — a jar instead of a bottle, a tin instead of a can. Use what you've got.

Can more materials be added later?

The site is built to grow — new materials and ideas can be added as the project develops.
