Plastic · 30 min · Easy

Toothbrush Holder

Cut and hot-glue three bottles together into a holder that stands on its own.

Watch it made

Tips and tricks

What you'll need

  • Three empty plastic bottles, same size works best
  • Scissors or a craft knife
  • A hot glue gun and glue sticks
  • A permanent marker, for marking cut lines
  • Paint, washi tape, or stickers to decorate (optional)

Instructions

01

Wash and dry all three bottles, and peel off any labels.

02

Decide how tall you want the holder, then mark a cutting line around each bottle at that height with the marker.

03

Carefully cut each bottle along the marked line — ask an adult for a hand if you are using a craft knife.

04

Arrange the three cut bottle bases side by side, however you would like the finished holder to look.

05

Run hot glue along the touching sides of the bottles and press them firmly together, holding for a few seconds until set.

06

Let the glue fully cool for a few minutes before using.

07

Decorate with paint, washi tape, or stickers if you like.
