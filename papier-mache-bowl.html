Papier-Mache Bowl

Layer strips over a mould for a bowl that is dry by morning.

Watch it made

Video goes here

What you'll need

  • Newspaper or scrap paper, torn into strips
  • Flour and water (or diluted PVA glue) for paste
  • A bowl to use as a mould
  • Plastic wrap or petroleum jelly
  • Scissors
  • Paint, for decorating once dry (optional)

Instructions

01

Cover the outside of a bowl with plastic wrap, or a thin layer of petroleum jelly, so the papier-mache does not stick.

02

Mix flour and water into a smooth, thin paste — or use diluted PVA glue instead.

03

Dip paper strips into the paste and layer them over the outside of the bowl, smoothing out any air bubbles.

04

Build up three to four layers, letting each layer dry slightly before adding the next.

05

Leave the whole thing to dry overnight in a warm, dry spot.

06

Once fully dry and hard, gently ease the papier-mache shell off the mould.

07

Trim the rim with scissors, then paint or decorate if you like.
